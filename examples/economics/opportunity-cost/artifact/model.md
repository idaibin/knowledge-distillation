# Opportunity Cost

## Core model

The relevant cost of a choice is the value of the best feasible alternative forgone. It is not every rejected option added together.

## What can count as forgone value

- Money
- Time
- Flexibility
- Learning
- Risk exposure

## Boundary

First establish which alternatives are genuinely feasible. Do not replace future tradeoffs with past irreversible spending.

## Open questions

- Which alternative is actually the next best option?
- How uncertain are its future benefits?
