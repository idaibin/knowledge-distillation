# Four-part delay diagnosis

## Problem

A team repeatedly misses delivery commitments.

## Steps

1. Restate the measurable outcome and deadline; check whether responsible people describe the same result.
2. Identify the accountable owner and decision rights; check whether tradeoffs can be resolved.
3. Compare work with skills, capacity, and dependencies; check whether the plan is feasible.
4. Inspect feedback cadence and escalation latency; check whether problems surface early enough to change the plan.

## Boundaries

- Use observable work evidence, not personality labels.
- Route legal, safety, harassment, and health matters to qualified processes.

## Anti-pattern

Do not diagnose motivation before checking the management system.
